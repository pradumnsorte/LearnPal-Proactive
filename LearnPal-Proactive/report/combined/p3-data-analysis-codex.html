What survives statistical checking?

The sample is small, so the page emphasizes effect direction, behavioral separation, and triangulation. Learning gains are real overall; condition-level learning differences remain underpowered.

Tests are exploratory: exact Wilcoxon for pre-post change and permutation Kruskal-Wallis for condition differences. P-values are evidence-strength markers, not final claims.

Interpretations worth carrying into the thesis.

These are the higher-level claims that the numbers can support without overstating the sample.

The missing interview data is the main next layer. It can explain whether ignored prompts were unread, quickly understood, or actively rejected.

The prototypes changed behavior before they clearly separated learning outcomes.

Feature engagement differs strongly by condition, while raw gain and normalized gain do not. This validates the design manipulation without overstating learning effects.

Agency is an outcome variable.

Intermittent's subjective advantage is not just preference language; it appears in control and low distraction ratings.

Interaction quality matters more than interaction quantity.

Raw feature volume relates weakly to gain. Feed answering and explicit keyword action are more meaningful signals than exposure count.

Continuous is the balanced condition.

It has the strongest final performance and a high level of constructive activity without the same interruption profile as Proactive.

Proactive is powerful but expensive.

It creates the fastest first interactions and largest raw gains from low baseline, but it also carries a control and distraction cost.

Interviews are needed for passive cognitive uptake.

The logs know what was clicked, skipped, or dismissed. They do not know what was read, understood, or mentally used.

Caveats that should stay visible.

Several exported fields are useful but imperfect. The dashboard avoids relying on known-problem metrics as decisive evidence.

Low N

With seven participants per condition, tests are exploratory and outliers matter.

Logging gaps

Proactive active-video seconds and pauses are zero in export, and Proactive quiz sheet rows are incomplete.

Acceptance-rate ambiguity

Some exported acceptance rates exceed 100%, so uptake is reconstructed from concrete event counts instead.
