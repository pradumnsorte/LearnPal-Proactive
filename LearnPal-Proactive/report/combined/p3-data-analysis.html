The headline finding

The three paradigms shaped learning and engagement differently, not hierarchically. There is no single winner. The data tells a trade-off story between learner agency, AI initiative, and cognitive cost.

Six interpretations the data supports

  1. The manipulation worked behaviourally before it worked statistically on learning. The prototypes produced visibly different interaction patterns and subjective experiences; the learning test is underpowered to separate conditions cleanly (n = 7 / group).
  2. AI initiative creates opportunity and cost at the same time. Continuous and Proactive increase AI-mediated activity, but the same volume that drives exposure also reduces perceived control.
  3. Interaction quality matters more than interaction quantity. Feature volume has only a weak correlation with learning gain (ρ ≈ 0.23). Action rates (e.g. proportion of feed questions actually answered) carry more signal than raw counts.
  4. Final score and learning gain answer different questions. Continuous has the strongest absolute post-test mean; Proactive has the largest movement from a lower baseline. These should not be collapsed into one "best" claim.
  5. Learner agency is a measurable outcome, not just a design principle. Intermittent's advantage in perceived control and low distraction is statistically detectable (Kruskal-Wallis p ≈ 0.04 on AI control), even at this sample size.
  6. The logs undercount passive cognitive engagement. Proactive keyword prompts may have been read and mentally processed even when dismissed. This is where the interview data, when added, will matter most.
The three LearnPal paradigms shaped learning and engagement differently. Intermittent produced the most controlled and least distracting experience; Continuous produced the strongest descriptive final performance and a balanced engagement profile; Proactive produced the highest volume of AI-mediated activity and the largest raw learning gains from a lower baseline. The results suggest a design trade-off between learner agency and AI initiative rather than a single universally superior paradigm.

Data quality & caveats

  • Sample size. n = 7 per condition. Effect sizes and CIs are more honest than p-values here.
  • I02 is a 41-second session. They completed all forms but barely used the prototype. Treated as data point, not as a representative case.
  • C06 has a 4,645-second session with zero quiz attempts — likely a partially-attended session (tab left open).
  • Proactive's active_video_seconds is zero by design — the Proactive prototype doesn't instrument the video_play/video_pause pair the same way Intermittent and Continuous do.
  • The exported ai_acceptance_rate_pct column exceeds 100% in Intermittent and Continuous and was not used; per-event counts are used directly instead.
  • The Proactive Quizzes detail sheet excludes 47 backfilled-sentinel rows (a known data-recovery from before the quiz-submit route was added). The Comparable totals correctly count those quiz attempts via the event log.
  • The interview component (Step 8) is not yet in the dataset. It is the most important addition for understanding the Proactive keyword-stream behaviour.
